How it works

PhishGuard runs a set of transparent heuristics — sender/brand mismatch, lookalike and punycode domains, raw-IP or misleading URLs, urgency and credential-harvest language, and mismatched link text. Each finding is weighted, summed into a 0–100 score, and mapped to a verdict. It's a teaching tool, not a replacement for a real mail gateway.
